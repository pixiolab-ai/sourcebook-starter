# Learning log

One entry per week, written before you tag. Keep it short and specific: it
feeds your Day 43 README and the stories you tell in interviews.

- **What I built:** what runs now that didn't last week.
- **One decision + evidence:** the options, your choice, and the number or
  observation behind it.
- **One thing that broke:** what failed, how you found it, and the fix (or why
  it's still open).

Notes the days ask for, such as the Day 2 token table or the Day 4 sentences
on attention, go under that week's three lines.
